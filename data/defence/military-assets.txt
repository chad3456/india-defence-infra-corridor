WHAT THIS IS

  What public catalogues list for each country, with the source of every
  line named. It reports what is published. It does not assess capability,
  readiness, posture or intent, and it deliberately produces no combined
  score across categories — those are the interesting questions and none of
  them is answerable from a catalogue. A single number implying otherwise
  would be the most misleading thing in this file.

THE FIVE SOURCES, AND HOW FAR EACH CAN BE TRUSTED

  SPENDING / PERSONNEL / WARHEADS
    SIPRI and the Federation of American Scientists, via Our World in Data.
    The strongest material here: standing research estimates, revised
    annually, each published with its own methodology. Spending is in
    constant US dollars and is not comparable to a headline budget figure.

  AIRCRAFT
    Volunteer-compiled catalogues on Wikipedia, drawing mostly on IISS's
    Military Balance and FlightGlobal's World Air Forces. A year or more
    behind, and those two routinely differ by tens of airframes on the same
    fleet. 'In service' means on strength, not available to fly. Where a
    force's quantity cells could not be read well enough, its total is
    withheld here rather than estimated.

  AIRFIELDS
    OpenStreetMap, objects tagged military=airfield plus civil aerodromes
    carrying a military tag. This counts what volunteers have mapped, so a
    country with an active mapping community looks better equipped than one
    without. Every count is a floor, never a survey.

  MISSILE AND ROCKET SYSTEMS
    Names from Wikipedia's per-country lists. A vocabulary of what a country
    is said to field. There are NO QUANTITIES in this source and none is
    printed: a named system may be one prototype or a thousand rounds.

WHAT THIS FILE CANNOT TELL YOU

  - Anything classified, which is most of what matters.
  - Readiness. Nothing here distinguishes an airframe on strength from one
    that can fly today, and the gap between those is the whole subject.
  - Where anything is based. No asset is resolved to an installation.
  - Quantities of missiles, ships, armour or personnel beyond the personnel
    total. Those sources were not read and are not guessed at.
  - Whether two countries' figures are comparable. They come from different
    compilers with different rules, and nothing here reconciles them.
  - Airfield counts for any country inside a failed sweep. On the build this
    file was made from, 1 regional sweep(s) never answered:
      russia-east — no answer after five attempts
    Countries inside those regions are undercounted by an unknown amount.
